# INKORA product requirements

INKORA is the brand that sells and maintains printing kiosks. Buyers are kiosk
owners, not platform administrators. The original kiosk remains separate during
the fleet rollout.

Owners must monitor only their purchased kiosks: printer connectivity, ink/toner,
cassette and rear-tray paper availability, jams, recent jobs, confirmed revenue,
refunds, fees, INKORA maintenance share, settlements, complaints, maintenance
history and service requests. One owner may purchase several kiosks.

INKORA administrators monitor every owner and kiosk, onboard and revoke devices,
manage pricing under the agreed policy, see owner-wise and total revenue,
track the planned 10% gross maintenance share, settlements and maintenance.
Owner isolation must apply to every query and action, not merely the UI.

Customer journey: kiosk QR, private PDF upload, print settings, immutable quote,
payment, assigned device printing and clear status/recovery. Ambiguous physical
output must never be blindly retried. Simulated completions are not revenue.

The owner control-room screenshot is the UI reference: business snapshot,
printer health/trays, recent jobs and complaints. Preserve the shared black,
cyan, violet and pink INKORA palette. Unknown hardware values remain unknown;
unconnected financial features must not display invented revenue or settlements.

Keep the legacy kiosk untouched. Never reuse its URL/project as the fleet runtime.
Cloud staging acceptance and real paid-printing production acceptance are separate.
